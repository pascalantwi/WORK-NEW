## Introduction

Greggs is a UK-based company that sells food, and it has around 2,700 branches (Greggs plc, 2026). Its profit went down by about 4%, and the cost of hiring staff also went up. The shops do not bake based on customer demand; they rely on their own guesses, which leads to leftover food. Using the information from everyday orders on the app, Greggs can predict what to bake in future, which will also help it plan how many staff it needs.

## 1. Value opportunities

People have less money, so they buy cheaper things. This means Greggs cannot simply raise its prices to cover higher staff costs, because it would lose customers.

When people order through the Greggs app, Greggs keeps more of the money. When they order through delivery apps like Uber Eats, part of the money goes to the app, so Greggs loses out.

Leftover food makes Greggs lose money, because that food could have been sold for a profit. Instead, it goes bad or is given away.
